How Sakina works

Three steps to your bed

1

Search & compare

Enter your dates and browse hotels by distance to the Haram or the Prophet's Mosque. Each room lists every broker's offer — the cheapest is always shown first, with nothing hidden.

2

Submit your request and the broker confirms, issues your invoice and voucher, and takes payment directly — cash or transfer. Sakina never charges you.

Transparent marketplace

Why do prices differ for the same room?

Hotels on Sakina are sold by licensed accommodation brokers. Several brokers may hold the same physical room for the same dates — each sets their own price, cancellation terms and extras like breakfast or airport transfer.

We show you every valid offer side by side. And because a room can only be sold once, the first confirmed booking locks it — after that, the remaining beds come from that broker alone. No double-selling, ever.
