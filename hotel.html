Rooms & broker offers

The same room may be offered by several brokers. The cheapest valid offer is shown first.

A physical room can be sold by more than one broker for the same dates — but only once. The moment the first bed or room is booked, the selling broker gains exclusive rights for those dates and every competing offer closes automatically. When you see an offer struck through, it means another guest booked this room first for your dates. Availability is always calculated from the physical room itself, never from the offer alone, so a room can never be sold twice.
